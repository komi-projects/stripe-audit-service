## Delivery Workflow

1. **Order received** (automated)
2. **Send intake form** (Google Form, free)
   - Stripe read-only key (or they run it themselves)
   - Company name
   - Team size
   - Current tools
3. **Run audit** (30 seconds, automated)
4. **Generate report** (automated)
5. **Send delivery email** (template, 2 minutes)
6. **Follow up in 3 days** (template, 1 minute)
7. **Upsell to SimpleRecover** (automated in email)
